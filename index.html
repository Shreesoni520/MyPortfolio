About

A builder who cares how it feels

I’m Shree — a frontend developer focused on expressive interfaces that still feel effortless to use.

I don’t chase trends for the sake of it. I care about hierarchy, pacing, and details people notice without knowing why: the way type lands, how a button answers a hover, when a page should breathe.

Right now I’m sharpening my craft in modern frontend — building personal projects, polishing motion, and learning how great products are structured.

How I work

From idea to something people can feel

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the goal, audience, and constraints before touching pixels or code.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Map layout, hierarchy, and content flow so the page has one clear job per section.

  3. 03

    Craft

    Build with clean HTML, intentional CSS, and motion that supports — never distracts.

  4. 04

    Polish

    Test on real screens, tighten timing, and leave something that feels finished.
